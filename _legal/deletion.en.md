# Account Deletion Request
**Effective date: October 20, 2026**

You can ask V-High to delete your Archmage Lugh account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Note:** Uninstalling the game does not delete your account data on our servers. Please send a request as described below.

## How to Request Deletion
:::box
Email [help@v-high.com](mailto:help@v-high.com?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request) with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include:
- Your Player ID (you can copy it from the in-game **Settings**)
- Your platform (Android or iOS)
- If linked, the Google Play Games or Apple account you signed in with
- A clear statement that you want your account and data permanently deleted

We may ask for more information to confirm the account is yours. If we cannot verify that, we may not be able to proceed.
:::

## Processing Time
We delete your account within **10 days** of verifying your identity and email you when it is done.

## What Is Deleted
- Your Player ID, nickname, and links to Google Play Games or Apple
- Game progress, characters, minions, gear, items and virtual currency (both paid and free)
- Settings, mailbox, and remaining rewards from quests, season passes, memberships and other ongoing products
- Usage records and analytics data linked to your account (except information already anonymized for statistics)

## What Is Kept, and for How Long
The following is stored separately for the periods below, as required by law or to prevent abuse, and then deleted:
- Records of contracts, withdrawals, payments and delivery of goods: 5 years
- Records of consumer complaints and dispute handling: 3 years
- Access logs: 3 months
- Abuse and sanction records: 1 year (to prevent re-registration to evade sanctions)
- Your deletion request email: 3 years, as a record of how we handled it

Information held by Apple, Google or other platforms in their own accounts (such as your store purchase history) is managed by those companies. Please contact them to delete it.

## Before You Delete
- Deleted accounts, progress, items and currency **cannot be restored.**
- Remaining paid currency and items are lost on deletion and are not refunded. If you have unused paid products bought within the last 7 days, please request a withdrawal under Section 18 of our [Terms of Service](tos.html) before asking for deletion.
- Remaining time and rewards on time-limited products such as memberships and season passes are also lost.
- If your account is under a restriction, the sanction record is kept for the period above even after deletion.
- You can reinstall the game and start a new account, but your previous data will not carry over.
