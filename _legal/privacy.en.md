# Privacy Policy
**Effective date: October 20, 2026**

V-High Inc. ("we", "us") protects your personal information in line with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information in our mobile game Archmage Lugh and on this website.

## 1. Information We Process
We process only the minimum information needed to run the game. We do not collect your name, phone number or postal address.

### Created or collected automatically when you play
- Player ID (a random identifier created when you first start the game) and nickname
- Game records: progress, characters, items and currency held and their changes, settings, language
- Device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, country and language settings, IP address
- Usage records: access times, in-game activity, crash and performance data
- Fraud-prevention information: restriction history, records of abnormal play detection

### When you link your account
- The account identifier provided by Google Play Games or Sign in with Apple
- With Sign in with Apple, your email address or an Apple private relay address, depending on your choice

### When you make a purchase
- Purchase records: product, time, amount, app store transaction ID and receipt
- Card, bank and other payment details are handled by the app store (Google, Apple, etc.). We never receive them.

### When you watch ads
- Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), IP address, ad impression and viewing records, collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) to serve and measure ads. On iOS, the IDFA is used only if you allow tracking.

### When you contact us
- Your email address, the content of your message and any attachments

## 2. How We Use Information
- Providing the game: creating and identifying accounts, saving and restoring progress, linking accounts across devices
- Paid services: verifying purchases, delivering products, handling withdrawals and refunds, answering purchase questions
- In-game offers: tailoring in-game offers (such as special deals) to your game progress and purchase history (for example, total spending and whether you purchased recently)
- Advertising: serving rewarded ads and measuring their performance
- Improving the Services: usage statistics, bug fixing, game balance
- Preventing abuse: detecting and sanctioning hacking, illegal programs and payment fraud, and preventing evasion of sanctions
- Support and notices: answering inquiries, sending notices, handling disputes
- Complying with legal obligations

## 3. How Long We Keep Information
We delete personal information without delay once its purpose has been achieved, except as follows:
- Account and game records: until your account is deleted. If you do not log in for 1 year, we may terminate the account and delete the data under our Terms of Service.
- Records of contracts, withdrawals, payments and delivery of goods: 5 years (Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
- Records of consumer complaints and dispute handling: 3 years (same Act)
- Access logs: 3 months (Korea's Protection of Communications Secrets Act)
- Abuse and sanction records: 1 year after account deletion, to prevent re-registration to evade sanctions
- Analytics data: deleted after the retention period set in the analytics service (up to 14 months), or kept only as anonymous statistics
- Support emails: 3 years after the matter is resolved

## 4. Deletion Procedure
- Information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been achieved is deleted without delay (within 5 days at the latest).
- Electronic data is deleted using technical methods that prevent recovery.
- Information we must keep by law is stored separately and used only for that purpose.

## 5. Sharing With Third Parties
We do not sell your personal information and do not provide it to third parties without your consent, except where the law specifically requires it, where investigative authorities request it under legal procedures, or where our business is transferred through a merger or sale (we will notify you in advance).

## 6. Service Providers (Processors)
We entrust the following processing to service providers, and we set and supervise the safeguards needed to protect your information.
- **Google LLC** (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics): account authentication, game data storage, usage statistics, crash analysis
- **Unity Technologies** (Unity In-App Purchasing): app store purchase integration

The following services process information independently under their own privacy policies, not on our behalf:
- **Google LLC** (Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing)
- **Apple Inc.** (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases)
- ONE store and other app stores (purchases made through them)

## 8. Automatic Collection and Personalized Ads
- The game includes SDKs for analytics and ads (Firebase, Google AdMob), which collect your advertising identifier and usage records. This website uses only browser storage to remember your language choice and does not use tracking cookies.
- Our advertising partner may use behavioral information such as your advertising identifier to show ads that match your interests.
- How to opt out: on Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads. On iOS, turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can still play, but ads may be less relevant to you.
- We do not show personalized ads to users we know to be under 14.

## 9. Your Rights
- You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, ask us to stop processing it, or withdraw your consent.
- Send your request with your Player ID to [help@v-high.com](mailto:help@v-high.com). After verifying your identity, we will act on it within **10 days** and let you know the result. You may also make requests through a legal representative or an authorized agent.
- To delete your account and game data, see [Account Deletion Request](account-deletion.html).
- Information that other laws require us to keep cannot be deleted, and requests to stop processing may be refused where the law allows. We will tell you the reason.

## 10. Children
The Services are not directed at children under 14, and we do not collect personal information from children under 14 without the consent of a legal guardian. If we learn that we have, we will delete it without delay. Outside Korea, the minimum age in your country applies (for example, 13 in the United States).

## 11. Security Measures

- Technical: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), server access control, access logs, protection against game data tampering
- Physical: use of data centers run by a security-certified cloud provider (Google)
